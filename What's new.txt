Advance Typer - What's New

* Translation Spinner:
Added a dedicated Translation Dropdown menu (Off, Manual Select, Urdu, English, Hindi, Punjabi, Arabic) to easily select or prompt translation targets.

* Sentence Ending Symbol:
Added a new setting to control ending symbols for finished sentences (Auto/Native Period, Space, Period/Full Stop, or New Line).

* Clean UI:
Cleaned up descriptions in Settings for a simple, clear, and readable user interface.

* Smart Structure & Spacing:
Enhanced AI prompts for long dictations with intelligent word spacing, sentence boundaries, and native language punctuation formatting.

* Voice Typing Labels:
Added explicit "Select Language for Voice Typing" label above the language selection spinner.

* Language Rules Preserved:
Reinforced strict grammar, spacing, and punctuation rules across Urdu, English, Hindi, Punjabi (Shahmukhi), and Arabic (with Zero-Emoji rule and proper Tashkeel).
